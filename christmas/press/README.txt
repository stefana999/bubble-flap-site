Bubble Dare: Christmas press kit
================================

Game: Bubble Dare, a one-tap flying game for iPhone and iPad
Developer: Stefan Johann Arngrimsson (Mothlark), Hafnarfjordur, Iceland
Released: November 2026. Free, with ads.
App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/bubble-dare/id6816718972
Website: https://bubbledare.mothlark.com/christmas/
Contact: support@mothlark.com

Christmas season: 1 December to 6 January, already in the game (no update needed).
- Snow settles on dead power lines and melts off live ones, showing which wires to avoid.
- Every world is dressed: wreaths and snowmen in the suburb, a tree downtown, lights on the desert's cacti.
- Geyser Valley, the game's Icelandic world: the thirteen Yule Lads arrive one a night from
  12 December, all thirteen by Christmas Eve, then go home one by one until 6 January
  (Threttandinn). The Yule Cat sits on the ridge after dark.
- Christmas dailies: Midwinter (21 Dec), Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, Boxing Day (24-26 Dec),
  New Year (1 Jan). One street a day, the same for every player.

Everything in these files is rendered by the game itself. Free to use in coverage of Bubble Dare.
