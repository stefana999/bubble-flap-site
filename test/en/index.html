  1. Day 1

    First look

    Install the game and play a few rounds. Answer the ad question the way you normally would. What didn't make sense straight away?

  2. Days 2–4

    Get the hang of it

    Pop bubbles for energy, let a bubble swallow you and tap fast to pop it from the inside, and keep clear of live wires. Try the daily challenge.

  3. Days 5–7

    Shop and postcards

    Collect pearls, buy something in the shop and change your bird. Look through the postcards. Fly until night turns to day and a new world takes over.

  4. Days 8–10

    Google Play Games

    Sign in to Google Play Games and open the leaderboards (Ranks). Play a round in airplane mode and check your score arrives once you're back online.

  5. Days 11–13

    Try to break it

    Turn the phone around, use the back gesture, leave the game mid-run and come back, turn the sound off. Anything that goes wrong is gold.

  6. Day 14

    Tell me about it

    Send me your feedback (see below). And keep the game on your phone until it's live.

Write feedback

What I'd most like to know:

  1. Which phone, and which Android version (Settings → About phone)?
  2. How many days did you play, and what's your best score?
  3. What was the most fun?
  4. What confused or annoyed you?
  5. Bugs: what happened, and where in the game?
  6. Would you keep playing? Why or why not?

Questions

I can't find the game in Google Play

Check that you joined the group and tapped “Become a tester” with the same account that's in Google Play on your phone. Sometimes it takes a few minutes, even a few hours, before the game shows up.

Does it cost anything?

No, the game is free. The shop also shows real-money purchases, but don't buy any unless I've put you on the test-purchase list. Then nothing is charged.

Can I delete the game after 14 days?

Please don't, not straight away. After the 14 days I apply to publish, and Google looks at the test while it decides. I'll let you know when the game is live.

Can I invite others?

Yes, please! The more the better, so it matters less if someone drops out. Just send them the link to this page.

I have an iPhone

Then you can get the game right now on the App Store. This test is only for Android phones and tablets.

What data is collected?

The game keeps your progress on your phone. Google's ads and Google Play Games use some information from your device. See the privacy policy for details.
