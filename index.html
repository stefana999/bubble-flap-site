How to play

One finger, four rules. Easy to pick up — and you'll keep coming back for one more flight.

Tap to fly

Every tap gives you lift. Stop tapping and gravity wins.

Pop for energy

Every tap burns energy. Burst bubbles to fill it back up.

Break free

Some bubbles pull you in. Tap fast to pop them from inside — the longer you dare to stay, the bigger the reward.

Mind the wires

Poles are fine to fly past. Live, sparking wires are not.

Small game, big sky
