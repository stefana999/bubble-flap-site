How to play

One finger, four rules. Easy to pick up, and you'll keep coming back for one more flight.

Tap to fly

Every tap gives you lift. Stop tapping and gravity wins.

Pop for energy

Every tap burns energy. Burst bubbles to fill it back up.

Dare the swirl

Swirl bubbles swallow you. Stay inside and the reward climbs from ×1.5 to ×8, but every tap to break out costs air.

Mind the wires

Poles are fine to fly past. Live, sparking wires are not.

A new world every dawn

Every run flies on from noon through golden hour into night. Make it to dawn and the next world rolls in, with its own sky, its own street and its own ways to end your run.

Start where you've been

Reach a world in a run and it opens as a place to start. Pick it on the Departures board and your next run flies the loop from there, so the far worlds are never far away.

Something to chase every day

Every run counts towards something: a bigger flock, a better multiplier, the next postcard or that hat you've had your eye on.

Gather a flock

Some bubbles hold a bird. Pop one and it follows you, up to six in a V with you at its point. They fly your path, dart for bubbles you missed, and a full flock doubles your flying score. Lose one and your run carries on.

The Daily Bubble

One street a day, the same for every player, with its own twist. You get three tries and your best one counts. Keep a streak going for bonus pearls.

Missions

Three missions at a time. Finish them to raise your score multiplier, all the way to ×10. Each kind asks for a little more every time you finish one.

Send a postcard home

Every world at every time of day is a postcard, with rare moments to find besides. Each card shows your bird in the outfit it wore, signed and postmarked. Pop 10 in a row in a place to earn its gold print.

Dress up your bird

Over 100 looks: birds, hats, glasses, capes and scarves, trails, pop effects and whole streets. Try anything on for free, then buy it with pearls you earn as you play. New picks every day, one of them on sale. Looks never change how the game plays.

Dressed for the season

The game dresses up for Halloween and Christmas, and prints its own edition of every postcard for each. No update to download: open the game and the season is there.
