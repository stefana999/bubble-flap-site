Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to flap. Flapping uses energy, so pop bubbles to refill it. Some bubbles pull you inside: tap quickly to pop them from the inside. The longer you stay in, the bigger the reward, but stay too long and it's game over. Flying past poles is safe; touching a live, sparking wire is not.

My scores don't show on the Game Center leaderboard

Make sure you're signed in under Settings → Game Center and that you have an internet connection. Scores can take a few minutes to appear.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app or change phones?

Your best score and settings are stored on your device, so deleting the app removes them. Scores you've posted to Game Center stay on the leaderboard and follow your Game Center account to a new device.

Which devices are supported?

Bubble Flap runs on iPhone and iPad and plays in landscape. The minimum iOS version is listed on the App Store page.

Can I play without sound?

Yes. Turn the volume down or switch your device to silent mode and the game plays silently.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet. Bubble Flap is launching on iPhone and iPad first.

How do I get a refund?

All App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Bubble Flap collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See our privacy policy for details.
